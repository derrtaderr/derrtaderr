### What's pinned, in the order revenue moves

**Signal to pipeline**
* **[signal-desk](https://github.com/derrtaderr/signal-desk)**. An intent signal arrives and gets enriched, scored, routed to an owner and drafted into outreach. Every decision can be inspected, replayed or refused, and nothing sends without a human approval bound to that exact draft.
* **[account-scout](https://github.com/derrtaderr/account-scout)**. Account research where every claim is bound to a source fetched in the same run. A claim without a citation never reaches the report.

**Customer revenue**
* **[revenue-sync](https://github.com/derrtaderr/revenue-sync)**. Stripe billing and product usage, or your BigQuery warehouse, written to the HubSpot company and then reconciled. When the CRM disagrees with billing, reconcile fails and names the account with both numbers. Verified live against Stripe, BigQuery and HubSpot.
* **[monday-pipeline-brief](https://github.com/derrtaderr/monday-pipeline-brief)**. The weekly "what changed" brief HubSpot can't produce. Slipped close dates, deals that went backward, deals gone quiet.

**Proof the agents are right**
* **[gtm-agent-evals](https://github.com/derrtaderr/gtm-agent-evals)**. Evals and regression for GTM agents. Deterministic rules, a fail-closed LLM rubric, and an autonomy gate an agent clears before it runs unattended.
* **[gtm-architect](https://github.com/derrtaderr/gtm-architect)**. A GTM strategy instrument that grades the evidence behind six decisions and names what's missing before it lets you plan.
